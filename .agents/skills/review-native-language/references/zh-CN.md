# `zh-CN` native editorial guide

Judge the text as contemporary standard written Mandarin for readers in mainland China. A correct sequence of Chinese characters is not enough: the narration and dialogue must have natural Chinese information order, collocation, rhythm, and social voice.

## Sentence and discourse

- Prefer topic-led or context-led information flow where Chinese naturally does so. Omit an already clear subject instead of repeating it to match English syntax.
- Check verb-object collocations, result and directional complements, aspect particles, classifiers, prepositions, conjunctions, and modal particles in the exact context.
- Watch dense stacks of `的`, long prenominal modifiers, unnecessary passive constructions, translated possessives, and abstract nouns used where Chinese would prefer a verb or concrete action.
- Check the balance of short and long phrases when read aloud. Simple Chinese should be compact and rhythmic, not a word-for-word sequence of short English clauses.
- Let context carry obvious causality. Repeated `于是`、`然后`、`但是`、`因为` or explicit moral explanations can reveal source-language scaffolding.

## Narration, dialogue, and names

- Narration should tell or show the event, not repeatedly summarize its lesson. At higher levels, avoid turning every page into one polished proposition followed by its proof.
- Dialogue must be speakable in the declared character's age, relationship, mood, and situation. Check particles, forms of address, ellipsis, indirectness, humor, and emotional restraint; do not make every speaker use the narrator's explanatory register.
- Inspect transliterated or invented names in context. Keep stable character identity, but use a localized display name that a Chinese reader can remember and pronounce naturally when the shared work permits it.
- A title should sound like a Chinese book title. Reject mechanically translated noun phrases, awkward adjective-noun combinations, and invented verb-object compounds that exist only to mirror English wordplay.

## Level-specific judgment

- At A-C, natural simplicity means familiar words, explicit action, clean referents, and useful repetition. It does not mean omitting necessary classifiers, producing fragments, or giving objects unnatural human actions merely to keep a sentence short.
- At D-G, allow connective variety, idiomatic dialogue, and controlled imagery. Do not flatten Chinese into uniformly short declarative sentences.
- At H-K, use precise and varied Chinese prose. Complexity should come from thought, scene, and syntax—not from bureaucratic nominalization, translated analytical formulas, or dialogue that sounds like a report.

## Common translationese tests

Ask these questions for every page:

1. Would a Chinese writer choose this verb with this object?
2. Is the subject stated because Chinese needs it, or because English did?
3. Is the modifier placed where Chinese introduces this information naturally?
4. Would the character really say the full sentence aloud?
5. Does the metaphor or personification work in Chinese without knowledge of another-language title or phrase?
6. Does the page advance a scene, or merely state an abstract conclusion?

When doubtful, consult modern monolingual Chinese dictionaries and corpora for the exact collocation and sense. Do not use isolated web occurrences as proof of normal usage.

## 在线参考入口

只保存以下入口和用途，不要把词典、语料、标准或文章正文复制进项目。每次审校都应打开当前网页并记录实际使用的页面 URL。

### 规范和课程

- [教育部、国家语委：语言文字规范标准](https://www.moe.gov.cn/s78/A19/A19_ztzl/ztzl_yywzgfbz/)：检查现行规范汉字、拼音、标点及其他国家语言文字规范。规范问题优先采用这里发布的 GF 或 GB 标准。
- [《标点符号用法》GB/T 15834—2011](https://www.moe.gov.cn/jyb_sjzl/ziliao/A19/201001/W020190128580990138234.pdf)：检查引号、书名号、破折号、省略号、括号以及句末标点等出版规范。
- [教育部：义务教育课程方案和课程标准（2022年版）](https://www.moe.gov.cn/srcsite/A26/s8001/202204/t20220420_619921.html)：判断课程阶段、阅读任务和语言学习要求；不能据此直接证明某个完整词已经学过。
- [人民教育出版社：修订版统编小学语文一年级教材教学建议](https://www.pep.com.cn/bks/xxyw/jzjd/202505/W020250531413853679016.pdf)：了解低年级阅读、积累、表达和交际训练的实际教学语境。具体字词仍须核验对应教材版本和位置。

### 词义、读音和用法

- [汉典](https://www.zdic.net/)：检查现代汉语词义、读音、词性线索、字形和基础用例。它是实用辞书入口，不是国家规范或完整的语体证据；关键结论应与规范或语料交叉验证。

注意：汉典入口使用 `zdic.net`；不要把相似域名当作同一来源。

### 真实语料

- [北京大学 CCL 语料库](https://cclcorpus.pku.edu.cn/)：检索现代汉语搭配、句法模式和不同领域中的上下文。选择现代汉语及与作品相符的文体；不要用古代、翻译或网络语料替代当代儿童叙事证据。
- [北京语言大学 BCC 语料库](https://bcc.blcu.edu.cn/)：比较多领域、文学、新闻和口语频道中的词频、搭配与上下文。判断对话时优先看口语，判断叙述时比较文学或相近书面文体。

语料只说明“出现过”和“如何出现”，不自动说明表达优美、适龄或适合当前人物。必须打开完整上下文；单个命中和总频次都不能单独支持 `PASS`。

### 原生散文和儿童文学语境

- [中国作家网：儿童文学作品推介](https://www.chinawriter.com.cn/404059/404075/index.html)：寻找当代儿童文学的题材、儿童视角、对话方式和叙事节奏参考。
- [中国作家网：散文新作品](https://www.chinawriter.com.cn/404015/404018/index2.html)：比较当代散文的信息展开、句群节奏和自然书面表达；先判断文章是否匹配作品的读者、时代和文体。
- [中国作家网：《寄小读者》与儿童本位](https://www.chinawriter.com.cn/n1/2020/1009/c404072-31885014.html)：理解儿童散文中的平视角、儿童经验、童真和简洁表达。它是文学评论参考，不是要求模仿冰心或任何具体作家。

这些页面用于校准自然度和文体判断，不得复制段落、改写现成作品或模仿某位作家的可识别风格。审校报告只记录页面 URL、所观察的语言现象和自己的概括。

## 中文查证顺序

1. 先判断问题属于规范、词义、搭配、口语、叙事节奏还是儿童视角。
2. 规范问题查教育部或标准正文；词义和读音先查单语辞书。
3. 搭配和语域同时查 CCL 或 BCC 的完整上下文，并选择匹配频道。
4. 叙事节奏、儿童视角或对话问题再参考同文体的原生出版页面。
5. 至少两层证据相互支持后再下确定结论；否则返回 `NATIVE_REVIEW_REQUIRED`。
