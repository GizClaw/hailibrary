# The Lantern Cart in the Fog

Rain ends over the harbor, but thick fog hides the hill road. A watchtower bell sounds far away.

Pippa lifts her ears. “That is Bramble’s bell.” Roan watches the faint tower light. “Bramble may need help.”

Pippa rolls out her homemade lantern cart. Pippa says, “I saved the shortest road in its map.”

The cart starts toward the low reed road. Pippa follows, while Roan studies the wet ground.

Roan points to water moving across the stepping stones. “The rain came after your map. This flood is not on it.”

The cart still shines its beam toward the shortest route. Pippa says, “Its map says this way is fastest.”

“The old route does not show today’s flood,” Roan says. He stops Pippa before she reaches the water.

One cart wheel sinks at the muddy edge. Pippa pulls the cart back while everyone stays on dry ground.

The upper path begins at Nima's orchard gate. Pippa says, “May we cross your orchard to reach Bramble?”

Nima opens the gate. “You have my consent to use this dry strip. Stay beside me, and keep the cart away from the seed beds.”

Fallen branches block the orchard lane. The tower light is still dim beyond them.

Pippa reaches for the drive lever. A wheel presses against a branch without moving it.

Nima raises one paw. “Stop. I allowed the dry strip, not the seed beds. The wet soil will not bear that cart.”

Pippa switches the cart to manual mode. The three friends compare small route models on dry ground.

Roan says, “These old pottery arches make a dry upper passage.” Pippa matches the arches to one of their route models.

Nima leads through the side gate. Roan keeps the group together, and Pippa guides the dim cart.

The fog thins near the watchtower. Bramble rings a brass bell from the sheltered doorway.

“I stayed inside when the low road flooded,” Bramble says. Pippa presses close to him. “I’m glad you waited here.”

All four dogs return along the dry upper route. The lantern cart rolls between them instead of leading them.

Back at the workshop, Pippa adds a large pause lever. She also adds route tokens so travelers can compare choices and record permission.
