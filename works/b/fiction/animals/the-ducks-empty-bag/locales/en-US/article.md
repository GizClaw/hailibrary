# The Duck's Empty Bag

Three friends plan a picnic. Pip brings a blue bag.

Mole brings bread. Rabbit brings apples.

Mole asks, “Why is the bag empty?” Pip says, “For later.”

Pip folds the bag under one wing.

The friends eat and laugh.

Wind scatters peels and napkins.

They pick up every piece. Pip holds the bag open.

The bag is full. The meadow is clean.
