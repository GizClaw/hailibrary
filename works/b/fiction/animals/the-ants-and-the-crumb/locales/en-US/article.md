# The Ants Move the Bread

Pip finds a piece of bread. It is big.

Pip pulls. The bread does not move.

Pip calls, “Help me, Dot!” Dot the ant comes.

Two ants pull. The bread still does not move.

Dot calls, “Come help us!” One more ant comes.

Three ants pull. The bread still does not move.

More ants come and pull. The bread moves!

The ants pull the bread home.
