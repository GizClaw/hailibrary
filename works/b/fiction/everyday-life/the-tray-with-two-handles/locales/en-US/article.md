# The Tray with Two Handles

Oren makes a red tray.

The tray has one handle.

He adds three empty cups.

The tray tips on a turn. Oren catches the cups.

Ada says, “It tips when you turn.”

They add a second handle.

Oren and Ada hold both handles. The tray stays level.

Oren sets it down. Ada says, “The cups stayed put!”
