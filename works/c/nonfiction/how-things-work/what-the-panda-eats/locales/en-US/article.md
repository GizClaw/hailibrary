# What Does a Giant Panda Eat?

Morning comes, and a giant panda is hungry.

Almost all its food is bamboo.

First, it bites a soft bamboo shoot.

Soon, that shoot is gone.

The panda pulls a thick stem close.

It peels the hard skin away. It eats the soft part under it.

Next, it gathers a bunch of green leaves.

At noon, the panda takes a nap.

Then it wakes and eats again.

It eats 10 to 14 hours a day. After a nap, it eats again.
