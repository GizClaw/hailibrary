# The Shadow That Fit

Nia cuts out a paper bird. Bo watches.

Nia holds up the bird. She says, “It will fly here.”

Bo puts the lamp close. A huge shadow spills off the screen.

“Only one wing fits!” Nia says.

Bo points at the lamp. He says, “It is too close.”

They move the lamp back.

Now the smaller shadow fits the screen.

Nia says, “The bird is too low.”

Bo lifts the paper bird.

The shadow rises. Nia says, “The show is ready!”
