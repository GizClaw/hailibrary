# The Boot Prints Pointing Home

Wet yellow-boot prints cross Jo's porch.

Every toe points toward Jo's door. Jo asks, “Who came here?”

Jo calls Sam. She says, “Look at this trail.”

Sam compares one print with Jo's boot.

Both have the same little star.

Sam points to the toe marks. They show where the boots faced.

Jo remembers the red wagon.

Before the rain stopped, she pulled it backward.

Jo's boots match the size and star marks.

Jo dries the porch. Sam parks the wagon.
