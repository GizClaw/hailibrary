# The Rooftop Shadow

A large shadow moves across the wall.

Squirrel cries, “It looks like horns!”

Rabbit studies the shadow's moving edge.

A beam of moonlight reaches the wall.

Rabbit points up. He says, “The light comes from the roof.”

The roof shape moves. The wall shadow moves too.

The friends walk around the cottage.

They find a spinning pinwheel.

The pinwheel blocks moonlight and makes the shadow.

Squirrel says, “It was only the pinwheel!” The friends laugh.
