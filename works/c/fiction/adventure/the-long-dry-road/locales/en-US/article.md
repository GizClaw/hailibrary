# The Long Dry Road

Badger straps a juice jar into its basket.

Mud covers the shortcut. A dry road curves around.

Badger holds the jar. He says, “That mud is slick.”

Badger takes the dry detour.

The jar stays upright on the long road.

Badger finds Tortoise beside a tipped cart.

Tortoise says, “One wheel is stuck between stones.”

Badger sets the jar down. Together they lift the cart.

The friends follow the long dry road.

The juice and cart reach the cottage safely.
