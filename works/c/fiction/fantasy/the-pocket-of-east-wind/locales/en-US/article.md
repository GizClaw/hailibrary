# The Pocket of East Wind

Mei gives Lin a green bag.

East wind curls inside one pocket. “Keep it closed,” Mei says.

Lin walks toward the cloud ferry.

The ferry waits with a slack sail.

Lin sees the waiting travelers. “They need the wind,” he says.

Lin opens the pocket. Wind fills the sail.

The ferry glides across the clouds.

One tiny breeze remains inside.

At home, the breeze curls above the dough.

The travelers return. Everyone shares warm bread.
