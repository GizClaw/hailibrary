# Moonlight Parcel

Mara takes a silver parcel. It must reach the observatory.

The raised bridge blocks the canal crossing.

Mara tries a narrow lane beside the canal.

Wind fills the lane with paper leaves.

A moon moth lands. The moth says, “Look at the water.”

Moonlight reveals the dry stones beside the canal.

Mara follows the stones. Her parcel stays steady.

Mara and Moth reach the observatory.

Mara gives the parcel to the astronomer.

A warm globe shines inside. Everyone smiles.
