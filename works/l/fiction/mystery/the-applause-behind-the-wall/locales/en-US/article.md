# The Applause Behind the Wall

Iona rehearses alone beneath the theater’s work lights. She faces the empty seats. “Tomorrow, every one of you will be full.”

When her speech ends, a sudden clap bursts from the side wall. Iona says, “Who is hiding there?”

She searches the empty seats and balcony, but finds no listener.

Ren checks the doors, curtains, and backstage hall. “Something starts that clap. Let’s change one thing at a time.”

Maia taps her drum softly. Dust trembles on the side wall. “That dust moved when we heard the clap.”

They compare each clap with nearby actions. Both happened after something moved backstage.

Iona repeats her speech loudly. The wall remains silent. Iona says, “So my voice is not the cause.”

Ren closes the heavy stage door. The wall answers at once.

Maia places her palm against the wall and feels a brief push. Maia says, “The door moved the air.”

Behind a panel, Ren finds a loose wooden louver. Its angled blades can guide air through the wall.

The closing door raises air pressure for a moment. That push knocks the worn blades together.

Iona grabs a roll of cloth. “Then I’ll seal the opening.”

Maia catches the loose end of the cloth. “Don’t seal it. This room still needs fresh air.” Iona lowers the roll.

Ren shows them worn hinge pins and a small soft stop. “New pins will hold the blades apart. Air can still pass.”

They replace the hinges and add soft stops. The opening stays clear.

Ren closes the door again. The shutters move apart without clapping.

Maia tests the emergency alert. A bright light flashes while a bell sounds.

Maia sees the flashing light before Iona and Ren turn toward the bell. Iona nods. “You saw the light first. We’ll keep both warnings.”

During the full rehearsal, fresh air moves quietly and both alerts remain clear.

The next applause comes from a real audience. Iona says, “This time, I know exactly why the room is clapping.”
