Bo is new in our class. I say hello and tell him my name. He has a map of the school, but the rooms have no names. ‘Where do we draw and paint?’ he asks. I listen to him and draw a pencil on his map. ‘Come with me, Bo!’

In the art room we make a funny paper friend. Bo draws an eye, an ear and a mouth. I draw an arm with a big hand. Our friend can wave! We smile at each other. I have a pencil to share with Bo. He uses it to finish the other arm.

**Mia**　Hi! My name is Mia. Can I help with your paper friend?

**Bo**　Sure. Hold this side of the paper, will you?

**Mia**　Like this? Look at his eye. What shall we make him say?

**Bo**　A good hand for waving. Can you help me draw a mouth?

**Mia**　There’s a nice wide smile. Listen! He says he wants to go home. With you or with me?

**Bo**　Let’s share. You take him today. I’ll take him tomorrow.

Mum and Dad bring a small mat to our family picnic. My brother and I sit down. Then my aunt comes over the hill with my uncle, my cousin and the baby. My sister tries to sit beside me. There’s no room for her feet!

Grandma opens her bag. Out comes a long blue mat. Grandpa and I put it down beside the small one. Now we have room for everyone. I write ‘Our big family’ on a card. My mother holds it up. My father takes a photo just as the baby waves.

We make a picture for our school hall. Mei paints the sea blue. I draw a white boat. Dan puts a brown bear in it, with a pink hat and a purple bag. A little black bird sits on the bag. Our bear has company!

What colour shall I use for the duck’s feet? I need some orange. We only have red and yellow paint. Dan shows me how to make orange. Mei paints a green leaf on the water. I draw the duck beside it. When we put the picture on the wall, our teacher looks for the bear. ‘There!’ says Mei. ‘Look for the pink hat!’
