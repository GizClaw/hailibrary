**The Elm Picture House Programme**

The Short Walk is a 55-minute film about a girl who takes her grandfather’s dog to the beach for the first time. We’re showing it twice on Saturday. Both screenings are free.

**2.00 p.m. · Screen Two**\
The film is in English. This screening has no subtitles. After the film, you can stay for a ten-minute talk with the person who made it.

**4.20 p.m. · Screen One**\
The film is in English. You can also read the English words at the bottom of the screen. These are called subtitles. There is no talk after this screening.

For either Saturday screening, book online and collect your tickets from the desk at least twenty minutes before the film starts. After that, we can give them to people who are waiting.

**A message from Luz to Benji**

I’m free after three. Can we go together? I’d like to read the words while I watch. I need to leave the cinema by 5.30 for dinner at my aunt’s.

## Questions

1. Which screening can Luz and Benji go to together?
2. Why must someone collect their tickets by four?

## Answers

1. The 4.20 p.m. screening in Screen One. It starts after three, has English words on the screen and ends at 5.15 p.m.
2. Free tickets must be collected at least twenty minutes before the film starts.

Hello Benji Vale. We've saved two seats for you for The Short Walk on Saturday at 4.20 p.m. Nothing to pay.

Your code is ELM 72. Show it on your phone or write it down. You can ask a guest to collect both tickets. They will need the code and the name you used when you booked. You don’t need to bring the card you used to set up your cinema account.

Come to the ticket desk in the main hall by 4.00 p.m. Both tickets are under the same code, so please collect them together. Both of you will need to show a ticket at the door to Screen One.

**Benji** My sister’s football game is still going. I’ll get to the cinema at about four ten. Can you take my code to the desk? It’s ELM 72.

**Snacks and drinks at the Elm**

**Can I bring my own water?**\
Yes. Bring a bottle with a lid. If it’s empty, you can get free tap water at the small table near the toilets. Ask the member of staff at the table to fill it.

**Which queue should I join at the snack counter?**\
Use the left queue if you have already paid for your snack online. Show the number in your email. Use the right queue to buy something new. Staff at either till can tell you what's in a snack.

**Will there be popcorn after four?**\
We usually sell popcorn all afternoon. This Saturday, we have a small supply. If the popcorn sells out, we'll still have apples and warm rolls. The counter closes at five.

**4.05 p.m. · At the counter**

Tilda has brought an empty water bottle. Sami wants to buy a snack and hasn’t paid for one online. The menu board says, “Sorry, no popcorn left today.”

## Questions

## Answers

1. The small table near the toilets. A member of staff there can fill her bottle.
2. The right queue to buy a new snack. He can get an apple or a warm roll.

**Before the film in Screen One**

Your row and seat number are on your ticket. The letter comes first. In C7, for example, C is the row and seven is your seat in that row.

Need a break during the film? You can leave at any time. Take your ticket with you, on paper or on your phone. When you come back, wait with the usher by the door so they can show you to your seat. During a dark scene the usher may ask you to wait for a brighter moment. Please keep your phone light off in the room.

There are seats and drinking water in the lobby if you want to sit outside.

**Five minutes into the film**

The room has been very dark since the film began. Kito wants to go out and get a drink of water. His throat feels dry. He plans to come back to the same seat, but he can't remember whether he's in row C or row G.

## Questions

1. What should Kito take with him when he leaves the room?
2. Why might Kito have to wait before going back to his seat?

## Answers

1. His ticket, on paper or on his phone. It shows his row and seat number.
2. The first few minutes of the film have been very dark. The usher might wait for a bright part before taking him back.

**Help us fill the Elm’s ticket box**

This Sunday the Elm is collecting old paper tickets. We're making a display for the café wall. It will stay up for a month. There is space for one ticket from each person. You can bring a copy if you want to keep the original at home. Before you hand it in, cut off any part with a booking number or your full name.

Make a label on a small card. Write one thing you remember about that visit. You can put your first name on the card. Write KEEP if your ticket can stay with us. Choose RETURN to get it back from the desk at the end of the month. We'll give you a collection slip when you hand it in. Put your card and ticket together in the box with our helper by the café clock.

**Example from the café wall**

I was here in 2009 when the lights went out in the middle of the film. We all sang one song while we waited. The film took longer than usual that night! Kemi. KEEP.

**Indi’s ticket**

Indi has found an old printed ticket with both her full name and her booking number on it. She wants to keep the original.

## Questions

1. What should Indi bring if she wants to keep the original, and which details should she cut off?
2. What happened during Kemi's film, and what should staff do with her ticket after the display?

## Answers

1. She should bring a copy and cut off the parts with her full name and her booking number.
2. The lights went out, and the audience sang while they waited. Kemi wrote KEEP, so the cinema can keep her ticket.

**Help choose our next weekend film**

**Night Wheels · 6.30 p.m.**\
A film about a boy who fixes bikes with his grandad. Stay after the film to ask the makers a question. With the questions afterwards, you'll be here for about ninety minutes.

**Paper City · 11.00 a.m.**\
An animation about two friends who make a town out of paper. We will put out scissors and card after the film so you can make one of the buildings. Allow two hours for the film and activity.

Vote at the desk by Sunday at five or use the cinema website by eight on Sunday evening. We hope to show the winner next month. We're still waiting to hear which screen we can use. Check our website a week after the vote closes. We’ll put the date there if a room is free.

**A note from Laz**

Can you remind me to vote when I get home at seven on Sunday? I can only go in the daytime. I like the sound of the bike film, but I’d love to stay and make something from paper. I have a whole box of card at home and never know what to do with it.

## Questions

1. Which film should Laz vote for? Give two reasons.
2. How can Laz vote at seven, and when can he check the date of the winning film?

## Answers

1. Paper City. It is in the daytime and has an activity where Laz can make things out of paper.
2. Laz can vote on the website at seven. A week after voting closes, the website will give a date if the cinema has a screen free.
