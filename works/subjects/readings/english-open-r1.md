Lin puts a red box on the table. Bo puts his ear against the lid. “Is it a clock?” he asks. Lin gives the box a little shake. Something rings inside. She lifts the red lid. There in the box is a tiny bell for their class bookshop.

## Questions

1. Where does Lin put the box?
2. What makes the sound inside the box?

## Answers

① On the table. ② A tiny bell.

Bo opens the red box. “Look! One bell,” he says. “Can we find one more?” He looks on his desk and opens a drawer. Under a sock he finds his bike bell. He puts it next to the box. Lin rings her little bell. Bo rings his. Ding, ding!

## Questions

1. Where does Bo find his bell?
2. What kind of bell does Bo bring to the shop?

## Answers

① In his desk drawer, under a sock. ② A bike bell.

## Questions

## Answers

Mei looks under the table for the red box. A big chair is in the way. “Can we move this?” she asks. Bo takes his bag off the chair. Together they move it back under his desk. “There you are!” Mei pulls out the box. She can just see her face in the shiny bell.

## Questions

1. What stops Mei from reaching the box?
2. Where do they move the chair?

## Answers

① A big chair. ② Back under Bo's desk.

The shop is ready. Mei brings a book about a dog. Tao holds the door for her. “Let's share it,” he says. Mei puts the book on the red box. She brings up a chair. They sit side by side. “I'll take this page,” says Mei. “You can take the next.” Look at the dog! He has a bone to share.

## Questions

1. What does Mei bring to the shop?
2. How do Mei and Tao share the reading?

## Answers

① A book about a dog. ② They take turns reading a page.
