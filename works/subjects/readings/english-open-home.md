Jia comes home with her little brother An. They kick off their shoes by the door. Dad tries to come in. Bump! “The bench is low,” says Jia. “We need a low basket.” She puts one under the bench. An drops his shoes in. This time Dad can open the door all the way.

## Questions

1. Why does Jia bring a basket?
2. Where does Jia put the basket?

## Answers

① The shoes are blocking the door. ② Under the bench.

An hangs his coat by the door. The room is warm, but it's cold by the window. He likes to sit there and watch the buses. Jia brings him his coat. “My knees are cold,” An says. He pulls it over his legs. Jia sits down just in time to see a big red bus.

## Questions

1. Why does An want to sit by the window?
2. How does An keep his legs warm?

## Answers

① He likes to watch the buses. ② He pulls his coat over them.

Jia gives An a piece of paper. “Fold it like this,” she says. An folds it and makes a little boat. Jia makes a boat, too. Jia puts a red dot on hers. Then they set both boats in a bowl of water. An takes a big breath and blows. The boats go to the other side of the bowl.

## Questions

1. Where do the boats float?
2. How can An tell which boat is Jia's?

## Answers

① In a bowl of water. ② Hers has a red dot.

Dad washes some fruit. He cuts a pear into long pieces. He gives one piece to Jia and one to An. An looks at his fruit. It is too big, so Dad cuts it again. Jia takes two clean plates to the window. An sets two pieces side by side on his plate. “Look! Rabbit ears,” he says.

## Questions

1. Why does Dad cut the fruit again?
2. What do the two pieces of pear look like to An?

## Answers

① One piece is too big for An. ② Rabbit ears.

An knocks over his cup. Water runs off the table onto the floor. Jia moves the cup away from the edge. “I'll get a clean cloth,” she says. An puts the cloth on the wet floor. The water soaks into it. An holds it up. “It's heavy now!”

## Questions

1. Why is the floor wet?
2. Where does the water on the floor go?

## Answers

① Water spills from An's cup. ② It soaks into the cloth.

An puts his coat on the mat. He sits on it and feels warm. Jia brings a cushion. “Sit here with me,” says An. Jia puts her cushion beside him. They share the mat. “Is there room for me?” asks Dad. Jia moves her cushion a little. There's a warm spot in the middle, just for him.

## Questions

1. What do Jia and An put on the mat?
2. How does Jia make room for Dad?

## Answers

① An’s coat and a cushion. ② She moves her cushion a little.

The room gets dark. Dad turns on a small lamp. Jia puts her hands in the light. She crosses her thumbs and moves her fingers. A dark bird flies across the wall. An puts one hand by the lamp. He moves two fingers up and down. On the wall, a mouth opens and shuts. “Hello, bird!” says Dad as the mouth opens.

## Questions

1. How does Jia make a bird on the wall?
2. What does An make?

## Answers

① She crosses her thumbs in the light and moves her fingers. ② A mouth that opens and shuts.

Dad turns off the lamp by the window. The room is quiet. An goes to bed, but he does not want the door shut. “I want to hear you,” he tells Jia. She waits by the bed while he gets his bear. Then Jia leaves the door open a little. “Good night,” she says in a quiet voice. Jia sits in the hall with her book. From his bed, An can hear her turn a page.

## Questions

1. Why does Jia leave the door open a little?
2. What can An hear from his bed?

## Answers

① An wants to hear her. ② A page turning in Jia's book.
