Grandma lives far away, so we send her four cards a year. In spring, we go on a picnic and draw the lovely pink flowers on a card. In summer, we go swimming, then paint a blue pool on the next card. In autumn, we pick apples. I tuck a yellow leaf into the envelope. It breaks along the fold, so I paint the next leaf instead.

In winter, we make a snowman. We write Grandma's name in the snow beside it. Winter is my favourite season because Grandma sends a card back just as our vacation starts. Her snowman has glasses. He looks a lot like Grandpa!
