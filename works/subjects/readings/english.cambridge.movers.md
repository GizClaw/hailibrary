On Saturday I went to my aunt and uncle’s flat. My grandparents were there, too. My aunt gave me a small box. “It’s a present for the new baby,” she said. “Can you take it upstairs?”

I took the box upstairs, but the baby was asleep. I put it on a chair on the balcony and went downstairs. A little later, my grandfather asked about the box. We went upstairs together, but the chair was empty! Then I saw it on the floor, under a blanket. I was surprised. Just then Grandma came back to the balcony. “I moved the box so I could sit down,” she said. “The blanket fell off my knees and covered it!” “Let’s give the present to the baby when she wakes up.”

Our class wanted to build a model bus. First, we made the bus from a big box. Next, we made wheels out of thick card. I made four squares. We put two pencils through the box and fixed a square to each end.

We tried to move the bus, but the wheels did not go round. “There’s a mistake,” I said. My friend had an idea. She put a cup on the card and drew round it. Now we had a circle. The new wheels were a different shape. It was easy to fix the bus. At the end of the lesson, our model went from one desk to the next!
