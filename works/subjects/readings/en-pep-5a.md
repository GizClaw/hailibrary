Ms Yu is our new art teacher. She is young, with bits of blue paint on the ends of her fingers. We all know her rule. She is strict about brushes. We must finish washing them before we go.

On Friday an old man comes to our class. He brings a small wooden chair for the reading corner. He is shy, so Ms Yu sits beside him and asks about the chair.
