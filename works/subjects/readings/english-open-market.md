Ning goes to the market with Gran. They leave home early to see the market open. The shop doors are still shut. At one table a woman is putting out her fruit. Ning asks why she is there so early. “Look,” says the woman. “My first orange is ready. Now I just need a customer!” Gran gives Ning a basket. This is his first morning at the market.

## Questions

1. Why do Ning and Gran arrive early?
2. What does Gran give Ning to carry?

## Answers

① They want to see the market open. ② A basket.

Ning sees oranges on a low table at the market. “Heavy for such a little fruit!” he says. Gran buys a few. The woman puts each orange into a bag. Ning tries to lift it. Now the whole bag is heavy. He takes one handle and Gran takes the other. Off they go together.

## Questions

1. What makes the bag heavy?
2. How do Ning and Gran carry it?

## Answers

① There are several oranges in it. ② They each hold one handle of the bag.

At the market, Gran wants to buy tofu. Ning waits with her behind a man. The woman sells the last piece to him. Gran smiles. “We can buy it next time.” “I sell it early,” the woman says. Ning looks at the empty tray. “Next time, let's buy the tofu before the oranges.”

## Questions

1. What will Ning do first on his next visit?
2. Who gets the last piece today?

## Answers

① Go to the tofu table. ② The man in front of them.

Gran gives Ning a coin to buy a little bun. Ning holds the coin in his hand while a girl pays for two buns. When the woman turns to him, Ning opens his hand to pay. “Your coin is warm,” she says. She slips his bun into a paper bag. Now there's something else warm in Ning's hand.

## Questions

1. What does Ning buy?
2. Why is his coin warm?

## Answers

① A little bun. ② He holds it in his hand while he waits.

A man at the market sells mint in small pots. “Smell this,” says Gran. Ning rubs a fresh green leaf. The smell makes him think of rain in his garden. He sees another pot with dry leaves. He chooses the mint with fresh leaves. “Can we buy this one for my window?” he asks. Gran tucks the pot into her basket. Ning's fingers still smell of mint.

## Questions

1. What does the mint smell like to Ning?
2. Which mint does he choose?

## Answers

① Rain in his garden. ② The mint with fresh green leaves.

Gran puts the vegetables in a bag. Ning sets the bag on the floor of the market. He starts to put the heavy oranges on top. One green leaf bends. “Oh! Let's try again.” Out come the vegetables. In go the oranges. Gran puts the vegetables back on top. This time the leaves stand up.

## Questions

1. Why do they move the vegetables?
2. Where do they put the oranges after the move?

## Answers

① To keep the oranges from crushing the leaves. ② At the bottom of the bag.

Ning and Gran leave the market. They stop by a blue shop door at the corner of the street. Ning looks back. He sees a blue cloth on a table. The blue cloth is much brighter than the door. “Next time I'll look for that blue table!” he says. He waits with Gran. When the green walking light comes on, they look both ways and cross the street.

## Questions

1. What helps Ning find his way?
2. What do Ning and Gran do before they cross?

## Answers

① The blue cloth on the market table. ② They wait for the green walking light and look both ways.

Before lunch, Gran helps Ning cut an orange. He tastes a piece. “It tastes sweet,” he says. He puts one piece on Dad’s plate so Dad can taste it, too. He sets his pot of fresh mint by the window. When Dad comes home for lunch, he eats the orange and asks for another piece. Ning brings him the bowl.

## Questions

1. Why does Ning put one piece on Dad’s plate?
2. What does Dad do when he comes home?

## Answers

① He wants Dad to taste the orange too. ② He eats his piece and asks for another.
