# The Northbound Lantern

Lia locks a bright signal lantern into the airship. It must relight the northern beacon.

Ren points at the clouds. “A storm crosses our route.” Lia checks the fuel and says, “Watch wind and fuel.”

The orange airship climbs toward the floating settlements.

A crosswind shoves them away from the beacon marks. Ren says, “We are drifting east.”

Below them, a mail glider rocks in the wind. Its pilot waves for help.

Ren checks their low fuel. “A long tow could stop both craft.”

Lia turns the wheel toward the glider. “We are not leaving her here.”

Wind shakes both craft while Ren clips on a tow cable.

The glider drags behind them. Their airship slows as dark clouds close in.

Lia opens the lantern housing. “Its core can start the glider.”

Ren connects the glowing core to the glider. Their signal lantern fades to a dull glow.

The two craft enter a calm gap in the clouds. Sena calls, “I can fly beside you now.”

The settlement appears. Without the core, its beacon stays dark.

Residents bring small lamps. They circle the dim lantern.

One by one, their flames feed the beacon. A large light blooms above the roofs.

Both craft leave under the steady light. Ren looks up. “The north route is lit.”
