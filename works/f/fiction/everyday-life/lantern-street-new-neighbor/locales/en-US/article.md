# A New Neighbor on Lantern Street

Amara’s family moved to Lantern Street. Mei wanted someone to play with.

“Want to play?” Mei asked in Chinese. Mei spoke Chinese and knew almost no English.

Amara’s family spoke Yoruba and English. Mei tried again in Chinese, but Amara did not understand. Mei let her hands drop.

Bean, Mei’s dog, rolled a blue ball with his nose. Amara smiled and rolled it back. It was a start.

Under the courtyard tree, Grandpa Lu shook his little bell. It made no sound.

“Still broken,” he told Mei. She watched his hand go up and down.

“Can I borrow the bell?” Mei asked him. Grandpa Lu held it out.

Mei raised the bell, then rolled the ball to Amara. Both girls could see the bell. It showed whose turn it was without words.

Amara caught the ball and waited. Mei held out the bell to her.

Amara pointed to herself, then to the bell. Mei nodded and gave it to her. Amara raised the bell and rolled the ball back.

They took turns with the bell and ball. Bean followed the ball from girl to girl.

The bell was still quiet, but the girls were laughing. Grandpa Lu moved his feet to let the ball pass.
