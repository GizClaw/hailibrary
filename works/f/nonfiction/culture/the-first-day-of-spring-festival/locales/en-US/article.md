# The First Day of Lunar New Year

On the first day of Lunar New Year, Xiaoyu had one question. What food did every family eat?

Xiaoyu and Mom visited Grandma first. “Happy New Year!” Xiaoyu said.

Grandma smiled and gave Xiaoyu a red envelope with money inside. “I have a New Year gift for you,” Grandma said.

Grandma set hot dumplings on the table. “Our family often eats dumplings on New Year's Day,” she said.

Grandma packed dumplings and joined Xiaoyu and Mom. Xiaoyu whispered, “I found the answer—it must be dumplings.”

Next, they visited Aunt Mei, Mom's sister. Bowls of tangyuan were ready. Her family often eats them on New Year's Day.

Xiaoyu waited to eat. “Which food is right?” she asked.

“Both,” Mom answered. “Some northern families eat dumplings, and some southern families eat tangyuan. Other families eat rice cakes.”

Xiaoyu made a two-step plan. First, she would give a New Year greeting. Then she would ask what each family ate for New Year.

After her greeting, Xiaoyu turned to Aunt Mei. “What did your family eat at New Year when you were young?” “We often had tangyuan,” Aunt Mei said.

Everyone shared both foods. Grandma chose tangyuan, and Aunt Mei took another dumpling.

On the way home, Xiaoyu held Mom's hand. She asked about next year. “Who can we ask about New Year food?”
