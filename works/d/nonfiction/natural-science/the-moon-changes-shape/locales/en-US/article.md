# The Moon Looks Different

Mina sets her sketchbook by the window. “I’ll draw the Moon,” she says. She keeps drawing for about a month.

On the first clear night, Mina draws a crescent.

A few nights pass. The Moon looks like half a circle.

Soon, Mina draws the full Moon.

After that, the bright part gets smaller.

One night, Mina cannot see the Moon. She leaves one page blank.

Her first Moon drawing was about 29.5 days ago. Now, a crescent returns. Each shape is a moon phase.

Mina puts all her drawings on the desk. “Did the Moon lose pieces?” she asks Dad.

Dad holds a ball so the lamp lights one side. The Sun shines on one half of the round Moon.

The Moon circles Earth. We see more or less of the bright half. Mina begins another row of Moon drawings.
