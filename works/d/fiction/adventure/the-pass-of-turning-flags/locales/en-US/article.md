# The Pass of Turning Flags

Qiao reaches a fork with three mountain paths. None has a sign.

A cloth streamer hangs beside each path.

The low streamer droops, and leaves cover its path.

The middle streamer whips sideways above an open ledge.

The high streamer stays steady between two rock walls.

Qiao studies the clues. She says, “The high path has less wind.”

Dan arrives with a basket of bowls. He asks, “Which path should we take?”

Qiao points high. She says, “Take this path. The rocks shelter it.”

They enter the wide high pass together.

A gust crosses the ridge. The rock walls shelter them.

They reach the pottery village with every bowl whole.

The villagers hang more plain streamers at the fork.
