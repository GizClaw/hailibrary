# The Last Winter Basket

One basket of nuts remains in Squirrel's shop.

Snow clouds gather outside. Squirrel whispers, “These nuts must last.”

Vole enters with an empty red bundle.

Vole says, “My shelf fell, and rain ruined the nuts.”

Squirrel looks at Vole, then at the dark clouds.

Squirrel measures the small basket carefully.

Badger brings two flour sacks. “I can trade these for nuts,” he says.

Squirrel divides the nuts into four equal bowls. Badger takes two.

Squirrel keeps one bowl and the flour.

Vole takes the last bowl. “I will bring fresh nuts in spring,” she says.

Snow falls outside three homes. Each neighbor has a meal.

Warm smoke rises from all three chimneys.
