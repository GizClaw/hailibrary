# The Tortoise and the Hare

A yellow ribbon hung between two trees. It marked the end of the woodland path.

Hare ran by Tortoise and laughed. “Should I wait for you?” he asked.

Tortoise looked down the path. “Let’s have a race,” she said.

They chose a red leaf for the start. When it fell, they went down the path.

Soon, Hare rounded a bend. Tortoise kept moving behind him.

By a tree, Hare stopped and looked back. He could barely see Tortoise. So he flopped onto the soft grass.

While Hare slept, Tortoise passed the tree and kept going.

A rustle woke Hare. His ears shot up. Tortoise’s nose nearly touched the ribbon!

Hare ran after her. Tortoise touched the yellow ribbon first.

“You won,” Hare said. He untied the ribbon and put it over Tortoise’s shell.
