# The Umbrella Rehearsal

Lina sets chairs beneath three umbrellas. A community play begins soon.

A shower wets Oma's empty chair. Lina cries, “The green umbrella leaks!”

Lina ties a cloth around the green umbrella.

Ben looks at all three and says, “Wait. Their angles are different.”

They set the umbrellas at equal angles above dry chairs.

Oma pours one can of water over the first umbrella.

She uses the same careful test on the other two.

All three chairs stay dry. Lina says, “The umbrella cloth is fine.”

Ben tilts the green umbrella. Water runs under its high edge.

Lina finds one loose foot on the umbrella stand.

Lina and Ben tighten the stand. Oma holds the umbrella straight.

The upright umbrellas keep the audience dry during the play.
