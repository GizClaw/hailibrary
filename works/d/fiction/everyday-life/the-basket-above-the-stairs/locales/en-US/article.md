# The Basket Above the Stairs

Seedling trays wait below the rooftop greenhouse. Rain clouds are coming.

Etta carries one light tray upstairs, but many remain.

Arun points to a rope and empty basket. “We could lift them,” he says.

Ms. Vale checks the pulley. She says, “Stand back.”

She checks the knot. A red band marks the loading limit.

Etta stacks three trays on one side.

The load is uneven. The basket tilts near the ground.

Arun says, “Spread the trays across the bottom.” They balance the load.

The trays stay below the red band. Etta closes the gate.

Etta pulls from the clear area. The level basket rises.

Ms. Vale receives it at the rooftop landing.

Every seedling reaches the greenhouse without being crushed.
