# The Clockmaker's Rain

Nia opens the workshop door. Tiny silver drops fall inside.

Each shining drop vanishes when it touches the floor.

Oren studies a stopped clock while Nia catches one drop.

A small bell rings. The drop jumps from the bowl.

Nia watches the drops and the moving pendulums.

Then she finds an empty space among the brass gears.

Oren says, “I removed one noisy gear last night.”

Nia cleans the dusty gear while Oren holds the lamp.

They return the clean gear to the empty axle.

Because the clocks turn together again, the silver drops rise.

The drops gather into a bright cloud.

The cloud becomes a shining ornament above the moving clocks.
