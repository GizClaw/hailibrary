# The Echo Stones

Fox carries an amber lamp. Goat carries a silver bell.

Thick ground mist hides the friends from each other.

Fox calls from a flat clearing, “Goat, where are you?”

An echo returns from a curved rock wall.

Fox follows the repeated call but finds only old footprints.

The same echo comes back. Fox says, “That was my own call.”

Fox stops in a safe open place instead of running.

Fox remembers their plan and raises the amber lamp.

Goat sees its glow and rings the silver bell.

Fox follows the bell while Goat watches the amber light.

The two friends meet in the clearing.

They leave the mist together on a broad path.
