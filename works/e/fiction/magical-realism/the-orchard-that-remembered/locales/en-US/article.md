# The Orchard That Remembered

Isa picks red fruit from the orchard's old trees.

Jun arrives with unlabeled seed packets from far away.

Jun opens one palm. A blue seed rests there. “It may grow in dry weather.”

Isa gives it back. “These old trees have always fed us,” she says.

Then hot wind curls the leaves. Small fruit drops too soon.

Each day brings emptier baskets and thinner shade.

Jun sets the blue seed near cracked soil. Jun waits.

Below ground, old roots bend toward the waiting seed.

Root patterns rise across the soil. Isa whispers, “The orchard wants to try.”

Isa takes the blue seed. She and Jun plant it beside an old tree.

A blue-green shoot rises. Old branches shelter it.

Seasons pass. New and old branches weave shared shade.

At harvest, Isa fills one basket with red and blue fruit.

Isa gives Jun a mixed seed pouch. “Carry both. Another orchard may need them.”
