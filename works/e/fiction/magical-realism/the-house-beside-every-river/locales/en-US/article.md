# The House Beside Every River

Ruo follows the river path with her red tool roll.

A warm little house waits beside the water. Small wheels hide beneath its floor.

The keeper opens the blue door. “One wheel has leaned all morning,” they say.

Ruo opens her tools and sets the crooked wheel straight.

At dawn, the repaired house rolls along the river.

It stops by a village near a broad reed river.

Neighbors bring vegetables. Ruo laughs with them. “I could stay here,” she says.

That night, the little wheels turn toward a new path.

Ruo stops smiling. The keeper closes the door. “Other rivers need this house.”

Ruo picks up a smooth pebble. “This visit can come with me.”

The house reaches a narrow river in red earth.

New neighbors borrow Ruo's tools. Together, they mend a bench.

Ruo sets another pebble beside the first. Their colors make her smile.

The house sets off again. Friends on two hills wave goodbye.
