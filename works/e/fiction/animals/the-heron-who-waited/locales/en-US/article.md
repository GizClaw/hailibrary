# The Heron Who Waited

Early one morning, Nia, a young heron, walked into the pond. She wanted a fish to eat.

Small fish swam near a yellow leaf. Nia ran toward them.

Splash! Her feet hit the water. The fish hid in the plants.

Nia tried again from the other side. More splashes, no fish.

Mara, an older heron, stood still across the pond. She was still as a tree. The yellow leaf came near her.

“How can you hunt without moving?” Nia called. Mara watched the leaf.

“Wait for the water to be still,” Mara said. “Then take one quiet step.”

Nia raised one foot. She set it down softly and stopped.

The wind moved the plants. Nia wanted to rush, but she did not move.

The last ripple was gone. A small fish came back by the yellow leaf.

Nia put her head down slowly. Her neck shot forward. Her beak caught the fish.

After breakfast, the yellow leaf floated between the two herons. Nia stood quietly beside Mara.
