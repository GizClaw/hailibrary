# The Fastest Slow Cart

Mr. Liu points to a crate pile. “Deliver these before the market opens,” he says.

Miko spins his red cart's wheel. It turns freely around the axle.

Zara tests her broad blue cart. “Mine carries a bigger load,” she says.

Miko grabs two crates and races away.

Zara works slowly. She fits six crates into one firm stack.

Miko returns, but four crates still wait.

Zara rolls around a fruit stall. Her six crates stay steady.

Miko loads two more crates and starts again.

A loose crate slips. Miko stops and pulls the rope tight again.

Zara arrives with all six crates still stacked.

Miko's third trip brings the last two crates. The pile is gone.

Mr. Liu says, “Zara took one trip. Miko took three.”

“Your cart carries more,” Miko tells Zara. “And yours turns quickly,” she replies. They link the carts together.

Next time, Miko guides each turn. Zara steadies the load, and every crate arrives.
