# Where Does the Rain Go?

The storm has passed, and our teacher checks outside. Now our class can find where the rainwater goes.

We follow a thin stream across the school yard. The water runs down the sloping path.

But the stream splits, and we lose track of it. We cannot follow all the water at once.

“Can we check one place first?” Nora asks our teacher. We start beside the plants.

Some water goes into the soil where the plants grow. It fills tiny spaces between bits of soil.

Other water runs into a drain in the path. We stay back and watch beside our teacher.

A shallow puddle sits on the hard path nearby. We wait, but the water doesn't run along the path.

Our teacher sets a stone beside the water's edge. We will come back and check the puddle later.

In the afternoon, the puddle is smaller. A dry gap now lies between the stone and the water.

The sun helps puddle water become vapor, a gas we can't see. A little water can also soak into cracks.

“Did the puddle water go into the air?” Sam asks our teacher. She nods, and we stay a little longer.

Later, the last wet patch dries. The stone shows us where the puddle used to be.
