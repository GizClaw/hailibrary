# The Route Under New Snow

After a night of snow, the ridge relay station falls silent. The village can no longer receive weather reports from beyond the mountain.

The Trail Loom projects an eastern route for Nara and Timo. Its calm voice reports, “Eastern path: shortest stored journey.”

Vale studies fresh drifts where trail markers once stood. “Snow buried the markers last night. The eastern path may have changed too.”

Timo checks the projector and battery cart. “The Loom learned from many trips. Let’s check its choice before we reject it.”

Nara finds two blue stakes disappearing beneath an overhanging shelf. Nara says, “The markers remain, but the path around them may not.”

The Loom reports, “Eastern confidence: high. Many travelers used this path.” Those journeys left the pawprints now buried under new snow.

“The drifts broke the old path,” Vale says. “Or they only covered it,” Timo replies. Nara studies the ridge without choosing an explanation yet.

From a stable rocky lookout, they compare the short eastern shelf with the broad western ledge. Nara says, “From here, we can check both paths.”

Low sunlight casts a dark line beneath a thin bridge of snow. Vale says, “That shadow reveals an open gap.”

A route stake stands beyond the gap. Timo says, “So the Loom recognized the marker but missed the changed approach.”

Nara switches off the eastern projection and places a caution token by its model. “The Loom found the stake, but its trip records are buried under new snow.”

Vale says, “The western shelf is longer, but stone supports it.” Its broad turns also leave room for the battery cart.

Timo checks the partly drained battery. Timo says, “The longer path uses more power. We must travel steadily and save the projector.”

“Vale could go west while we search east,” Timo suggests. Nara shakes her head. “Splitting up adds another risk. We need one plan for the whole team.”

They dim the Loom and use it only at nearby junctions. The machine reports, “Local guidance active. Distant recommendation paused.”

Vale leads along the western rock. Nara walks beside the cart, while Timo watches its wheels and battery.

At a steep bend, everyone pauses to keep the cart level. Nara says, “One at a time. Keep the cart between us.”

Ora waves a silver lantern from the stone relay shelter. “You arrived together,” she says. “The storm loosened one connection inside.”

Timo clears frost from the loose relay contact and secures it. The signal returns before the wind strengthens again.

Snow thickens outside, so all four dogs remain in the stone shelter. Vale looks through the window. “The wind is rising. We wait here.”

After the wind eases, Nara builds wordless models of both paths. She points to each in turn. “This one records the eastern gap. This one shows the western ledge that fits our cart.”

They return together on the broad route. The Loom now follows their current observations instead of commanding the journey.
